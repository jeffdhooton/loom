# Loop-engineering brief — quality rubric (score 0..1)

Grade the brief against these subjective-quality criteria. The objective length
bounds (220–320 words) are enforced separately by deterministic checks, so judge
quality, not word count.

- States the core idea of loop engineering clearly in the first paragraph.
- Includes at least 3 concrete, specific, technical points (e.g. the iterate-to-a-goal
  cycle, cheap models making loops affordable, verification gates) — not generic filler.
- Contains NO marketing fluff, hype, buzzwords, or empty superlatives.
- Ends with one concrete, actionable takeaway the reader can act on.

Score 0.8 or higher ONLY if all four criteria genuinely hold.
