At its very core, loop engineering is all about loops — and loops, as everyone in the
industry already knows, are incredibly, tremendously, and undeniably important. A loop,
in the broadest and most all-encompassing sense of the word, is a process that repeats
itself over and over again, again and again, iterating continuously and relentlessly
toward some kind of goal, objective, target, or desired end-state outcome. When you
really stop and think about it deeply, almost everything in the entire universe is, in
some profound and meaningful way, a loop of one kind or another, and that is precisely
what makes loop engineering so powerful, so versatile, and so transformative.
